What I Do

I specialize in local SEO for service businesses — plumbers, HVAC contractors, roofers, electricians, dentists, detailers, and other local service providers across Flagler County. My focus is narrow on purpose: I'd rather be the best at local SEO for Palm Coast businesses than mediocre at everything.

My approach combines three proven pillars:

1. Google Business Profile Optimization — your GBP is the #1 factor in local rankings. I optimize every field, add strategic photos, post regularly, and manage your review strategy.

2. Citation Building — consistent business info across 40+ directories tells Google you're legitimate. I build and verify citations on the data aggregators, Apple Maps, Yelp, and dozens more.

3. Review Management — reviews are social proof and a ranking factor. I help you generate authentic 5-star reviews and respond to every one.
