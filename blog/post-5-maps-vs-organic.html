Google Maps SEO vs Organic SEO: What's the Difference?

Published: May 9, 2026 Reading time: 7 minutes

Your customer searches "Local SEO near me." Google shows 3 results:

  1. A map with 3 local businesses (Google Maps results)
  2. Below that, 10 organic search results (regular website links)

Most businesses chase organic rankings and ignore maps. That's a mistake.

Google Maps Results (Local Pack)

What It Shows:

  • 3-5 local businesses with map pins
  • Star ratings
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Website link

What Ranks Here:

  • GBP optimization (25%)
  • NAP consistency (20%)
  • Citations (15%)
  • Reviews (12%)
  • Distance from searcher (10%)
  • On-page SEO (10%)
  • Website authority (8%)

Timeline to rank: 30-90 days (fast)
Conversion rate: 75% of map clicks result in phone calls or visits
Competition: Lower (fewer businesses optimizing maps)

Organic Results

What It Shows:

  • Your website link
  • Meta description
  • Star rating (if you have reviews)

What Ranks Here:

  • Website authority (backlinks) (40%)
  • Content relevance (25%)
  • User experience (15%)
  • Page speed (10%)
  • Fresh content (10%)

Timeline to rank: 3-12 months (slow)
Conversion rate: 20-30% (lower than maps)
Competition: Higher (every business with a website competes)

Head-to-Head Comparison

Factor Google Maps Organic
Time to results 30-90 days 3-12 months
Effort to rank Medium High
Phone call rate 75% 20-30%
Competition Lower Higher
Best for Local services Authority/content

Which Should You Focus On First?

Start with Google Maps Because:

  • ✅ Faster results (30-90 days vs 6+ months)
  • ✅ Higher phone call conversion (75% vs 20%)
  • ✅ Lower competition
  • ✅ Easier to optimize (5-6 factors vs 20+)

Then Add Organic SEO Because:

  • ✅ Captures searchers who don't use maps
  • ✅ Builds long-term authority
  • ✅ Creates a moat against competitors

The Strategy

Months 1-3: Optimize Google Maps (GBP, citations, reviews)

Months 2-4: Start building organic rankings (blog content, backlinks)

Months 4+: Maintain both simultaneously

The Result

Businesses that own BOTH maps AND organic rankings:

  • Get 2x more phone calls
  • Capture searchers across all results
  • Appear as the dominant local player
