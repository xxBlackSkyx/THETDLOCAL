## Common Mistakes Towing Companies Make in Local SEO

Even seasoned marketers can fall into pitfalls that hurt local rankings. Avoid these mistakes to keep your towing business ahead.

### 1. Ignoring Mobile Optimization

Most emergency searches happen on mobile. A non‑responsive site can lead to a 60% higher bounce rate. Ensure your design scales to all screen sizes and loads in under 3 seconds.

### 2. Duplicate or Inconsistent NAP Information

If your phone number or address differs between your website, Google Business Profile, and online directories, search engines will flag you as unreliable. Use a single source of truth and verify all listings.

### 3. Over‑Optimizing Keywords

Keyword stuffing—repeating the target phrase over 10 times on a page—causes penalties. Aim for natural usage, with the keyword appearing in the title, first paragraph, and a few subheadings.

### 4. Neglecting Reviews

A 4‑star rating with 20 reviews is better than a 5‑star rating with 2 reviews. Encourage customers to leave feedback and respond to negative reviews professionally. Use the review response feature on your Google Business Profile to engage with reviewers.

### 5. Forgetting to Update Service Areas

If you expand to new towns like Bunnell or Flagler Beach, update your Google Business Profile service‑area list immediately. Search engines consider this data when determining local relevance.

### 6. Not Using Structured Data

Without schema markup, search engines may not display your business in the Local Pack. Implement `LocalBusiness` schema and test it with Google’s Rich Results Test tool.
