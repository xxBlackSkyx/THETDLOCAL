## How to Add Products to Google Business Profile Step-by-Step
Adding products to your Google Business Profile is a straightforward process that can be completed in a few steps. First, ensure you have a verified Google Business Profile. If you haven't claimed and verified your profile yet, you can do so by following the prompts on the Google Business Profile website. Once your profile is verified, follow these steps:
1. Sign in to your Google Business Profile account.
2. Click on the "Products" tab from the menu.
3. Click on the "Add product" button.
4. Enter the product name, description, price, and category.
5. Upload high-quality photos of the product.
6. Add a call-to-action (CTA) button, such as "Buy" or "Learn more," and link it to your website or an online store.
7. Click "Save" to add the product to your profile.
Repeat this process for each product you want to add. You can also use the Google Business Profile app to add products on-the-go, making it convenient to manage your profile from anywhere, whether you're in Town Center or along US-1.
