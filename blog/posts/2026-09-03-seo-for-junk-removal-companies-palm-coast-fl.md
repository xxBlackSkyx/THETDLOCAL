## Common Mistakes Junk Removal Companies Make in SEO

1. **Neglecting Mobile Optimization**  
   Many small companies still use desktop‑only designs. A mobile‑unfriendly site loses users and ranks lower. Ensure your theme is responsive and test with Google’s Mobile-Friendly Test.

2. **Duplicate Content**  
   Copying content from competitors or using generic service pages can hurt rankings. Craft unique, location‑specific copy that addresses local concerns.

3. **Ignoring Schema Markup**  
   Without LocalBusiness schema, search engines can’t easily identify your business’s address, hours, or services. Add schema to every page that describes your services.

4. **Sparse Keyword Use**  
   Over‑stuffing keywords can trigger penalties. Aim for natural placement—include primary keyword in title, first paragraph, and one subheading.

5. **Inconsistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone)**  
   If your business details differ across platforms, Google may flag your listing. Double‑check every directory and social profile.

6. **Not Soliciting Reviews**  
   A low review count or negative reviews can deter clicks. Set up a simple email follow‑up after service completion asking for a rating.

7. **Skipping Technical Audits**  
   Broken links, slow load times, or missing robots.txt can sabotage rankings. Schedule a quarterly audit using tools like Screaming Frog.

Avoiding these pitfalls ensures your SEO efforts deliver sustainable growth rather than short‑term spikes.
