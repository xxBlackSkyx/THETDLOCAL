## What is the Google Helpful Content Update?
The Google Helpful Content Update is a recent algorithm change aimed at promoting high-quality, user-centered content that provides value to searchers. This update is designed to reduce the visibility of low-quality, unhelpful content that's created primarily for search engine rankings. As a local business owner, it's essential to understand that this update affects not only your website's content but also your local SEO strategy. According to Google, the update is designed to "better reward content that is created to help users, rather than just to gain search engine rankings."
