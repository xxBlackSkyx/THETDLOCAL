## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Neglecting Mobile Optimization**  
   Many patients browse on smartphones, especially when searching “eye doctor near me.” A non‑responsive site can drive users away and lower rankings.

2. **Duplicate Content**  
   Replicating the same page across multiple URLs confuses Google and dilutes page authority. Keep content unique and tailored to each service.

3. **Over‑Optimizing Keywords**  
   Stuffing your content with the keyword “seo for optometrists palm coast fl” can trigger penalties. Aim for natural usage and focus on user intent.

4. **Ignoring Reviews**  
   A high volume of negative or unanswered reviews signals poor patient experience. Respond to reviews within 48 hours and address concerns.

5. **Failing to Update NAP**  
   Even a single typo in your address or phone number can break local citations and hurt rankings. Verify all listings after any change.

6. **Skipping Technical SEO**  
   Slow page load times, broken links, or missing sitemaps can prevent Google from crawling your site effectively.
