## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Ignoring Mobile Optimization**  
  Over 60% of local searches happen on mobile devices. A non‑responsive site loses visitors before they even call. Use responsive themes and test with Google’s Mobile-Friendly Test.

- **Duplicate NAP Listings**  
  Even a small typo—“Palm Coast” vs. “Palm Coast” (extra space)—can split reviews and dilute rankings. Conduct a quarterly audit of all citations.

- **Over‑Keyword Stuffing**  
  Stuffing “home remodeling contractors palm coast fl” into every paragraph looks spammy and hurts readability. Aim for a keyword density of 1–2% and use synonyms naturally.

- **Neglecting Reviews**  
  A lack of recent reviews signals to Google that a business may not be active. Promptly request reviews from clients and respond to both positive and negative feedback.

- **Failing to Update Content**  
  Remodeling trends change. Old blog posts or service pages can become irrelevant. Schedule quarterly content reviews to keep information fresh.
