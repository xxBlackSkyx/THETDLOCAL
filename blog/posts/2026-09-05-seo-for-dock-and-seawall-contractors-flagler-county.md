## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| **Ignoring Mobile Users** | 60% of local searches happen on mobile. A non‑responsive site loses traffic and rankings. | Adopt a responsive theme; compress images; test with Google’s Mobile-Friendly Test. |
| **Duplicate NAP Info** | Mixed address or phone data confuses search engines and erodes trust. | Use a single source of truth—Google Business Profile—and sync it across all citations. |
| **Over‑Keywording** | Stuffing “dock” or “seawall” into every paragraph triggers penalties. | Keep keyword density around 1–2%; prioritize natural, helpful language. |
| **Neglecting Reviews** | No reviews or negative feedback can deter prospects. | Promptly request reviews; address negative ones professionally. |
| **Missing Structured Data** | Without schema, Google may not display rich snippets (phone numbers, ratings). | Add LocalBusiness JSON‑LD to your site’s header. |
| **Failing to Update Seasonal Content** | Neglecting hurricane season warnings or post‑storm repair guides misses opportunities. | Update blog posts quarterly, especially before hurricane season. |

Addressing these pitfalls early keeps your SEO campaign strong and sustainable.
