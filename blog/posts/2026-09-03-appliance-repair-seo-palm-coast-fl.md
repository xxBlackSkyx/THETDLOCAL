## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even seasoned marketers slip on these errors, which can sabotage your local SEO efforts.

1. **Inconsistent NAP** – A name, address, or phone number that changes across sites confuses Google and erodes trust. Use a single source of truth.
2. **Keyword stuffing** – Over‑using “appliance repair seo palm coast fl” in meta tags or body text can trigger penalties. Keep density natural (1–2% is safe).
3. **Neglecting mobile** – A desktop‑only site will drop in rankings. Test with Google’s Mobile‑Friendly Test (https://search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly).
4. **Ignoring reviews** – Not responding or failing to solicit reviews signals poor customer service. Review management should be a daily task.
5. **Weak backlink profile** – Buying low‑quality links can harm rankings. Focus on local sponsorships (e.g., community events in Palm Harbor) to earn natural backlinks.
6. **Overlooking local content** – Generic national content doesn’t rank locally. Use local landmarks, events, or weather patterns to make content relevant to Flagler County readers.

Avoiding these pitfalls ensures your SEO remains healthy and continues to grow.
