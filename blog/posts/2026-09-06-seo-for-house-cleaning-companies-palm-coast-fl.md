## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Ignoring Mobile Optimization**  
   Many local businesses still use desktop‑only sites. Mobile users demand fast load times and easy navigation. A slow site can push users toward competitors.

2. **Inconsistent NAP Information**  
   A mismatch between your website and directory listings confuses search engines and erodes trust. Verify every listing weekly.

3. **Neglecting the Google Business Profile**  
   Leaving your profile incomplete or unverified limits visibility in the map pack. Add photos, business hours, and services promptly.

4. **Over‑Keyword Stuffing**  
   Cramming keywords into content can trigger penalties. Aim for natural usage and focus on user intent.

5. **Failing to Encourage Reviews**  
   A low review count or negative reviews can deter prospects. Request feedback after each job and respond professionally.

6. **Ignoring Local Content**  
   Generic pages fail to resonate with Palm Coast residents. Create blog posts about local events (e.g., “Preparing Your Home for the Palm Coast Summer Festival”) to attract community interest.

7. **Neglecting Technical SEO**  
   Broken links, missing alt tags, and duplicate content can harm rankings. Perform quarterly audits using tools like Screaming Frog.

By steering clear of these pitfalls, your cleaning business will maintain a strong online reputation and higher search rankings.
