Schema markup is a type of microdata that helps search engines like Google understand the context and content of your website, making it easier for potential customers to find you when searching online.

## What is Schema Markup?
Schema markup is a collaborative project between Google, Bing, Yahoo, and Yandex to create a standardized set of microdata that webmasters can use to markup their websites. This microdata provides search engines with additional information about your business, such as your name, address, phone number, hours of operation, and reviews. By adding schema markup to your website, you can enhance your business's online presence and increase your visibility in search engine results pages (SERPs). For example, if you own a restaurant in Palm Harbor, you can use schema markup to highlight your menu, prices, and reviews, making it easier for hungry customers to find you when searching for "restaurants near me" on US-1.

4. Test your schema markup: Use tools like Google's [Structured Data Markup Helper](https://www.google.com/webmasters/markup-helper/) to test your schema markup and ensure it's working correctly.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using Schema Markup
While schema markup can be a powerful tool for local businesses, there are some common mistakes to avoid. One of the most common mistakes is using schema markup incorrectly or inconsistently. This can lead to confusion for search engines and may even result in penalties for your website. Another mistake is not testing your schema markup regularly. Schema markup can be complex, and even small errors can prevent it from working correctly. Finally, be sure to avoid overusing schema markup. While it's tempting to add schema markup to every page on your website, this can lead to clutter and confusion. Instead, focus on using schema markup strategically to highlight the most important information about your business.
