## What It Is: The Mechanics of a Google Business Profile Suspension

A Google Business Profile suspension occurs when Google determines that a listing violates its policies. Violations can stem from misinformation, duplicate listings, prohibited content, or suspicious activity. When suspended, the profile disappears from search results, Google Maps, and the local pack, and the owner cannot edit or manage the listing until the appeal process resolves.

Key indicators of a suspension include:
- A notification email from Google explaining the reason.
- The listing no longer appears in local search results or on the map.
- The “Add or claim a business” option appears instead of the normal profile view.

Google’s help center outlines specific reasons for suspension, such as “business is no longer in operation,” “duplicate listings,” “inaccurate categories,” or “unverified ownership.” The exact cause is usually provided in the suspension notice, and that detail guides the appeal strategy.
