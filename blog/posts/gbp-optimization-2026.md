# Google Business Profile Optimization Checklist 2026

Your Google Business Profile is **the #1 ranking factor for local search**. Rank in the top 3 Google Maps results with this complete optimization checklist.

## 1. Verify & Claim Your Profile

- [ ] Verify your business through **phone, email, or postcard**
- [ ] Use the **exact legal business name**
- [ ] Link to your website
- [ ] Add correct phone number (NAP consistency critical)

## 2. Complete All Business Information

- [ ] **NAP**: Name, Address, Phone (must match website & citations)
- [ ] Business category (be specific — "Plumber" not "Contractor")
- [ ] Service areas (if applicable)
- [ ] Business hours (mark holidays as closed)
- [ ] Website URL

## 3. Photos (Critical for Rankings)

- [ ] 10+ high-quality business exterior photos
- [ ] 10+ interior/service photos
- [ ] Owner or team photos (trust signals)
- [ ] Before/after if applicable
- [ ] Recent photos (uploaded within last 90 days = ranking boost)

## 4. Services & Products

- [ ] List all services offered (with descriptions)
- [ ] Add service photos
- [ ] Set service areas with radii
- [ ] Price ranges (if applicable)

## 5. Reviews & Responses

- [ ] Respond to **100% of reviews** (both positive and negative)
- [ ] Aim for 4.5+ stars
- [ ] Encourage recent customers to leave reviews
- [ ] Monitor weekly for new reviews

## 6. Posts & Updates

- [ ] Post **2-3 times per month** (events, offers, tips)
- [ ] Use local keywords in posts
- [ ] Link back to your website

## 7. Q&A Management

- [ ] Answer frequently asked questions
- [ ] Encourage team members to contribute
- [ ] Monitor and update answers monthly

## Results

Businesses that follow this checklist typically see:
- **30-50% increase in calls** within 30 days
- **Top 3 Google Maps placement** within 60 days
- **Higher website click-through rate** from local search

**Ready to optimize?** Start with NAP consistency and add 10+ photos today. That alone moves most businesses up 2-3 positions.
