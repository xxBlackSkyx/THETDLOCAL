## Common Mistakes to Avoid in Small Business SEO
When it comes to small business SEO, there are several common mistakes to avoid. These include:

* Lack of content: Failing to create high-quality, engaging content can make it harder to attract and retain website visitors.
* Ignoring local SEO: If you're a local business, it's essential to optimize your website for local keywords and phrases.
