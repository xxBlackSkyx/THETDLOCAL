## How to Do SEO for Wedding Photographers in Flagler County: Step‑by‑Step

### Step 1 – Audit Your Current Presence

- **Website audit**: Check load speed, mobile friendliness, and existing keyword usage. Tools like Google PageSpeed Insights or GTmetrix help.  
- **Google Business Profile audit**: Verify that your address matches the one on your website, and that you have up-to-date hours and a high‑resolution profile photo.  
- **Citation audit**: List every online directory where your business appears. Use a spreadsheet to track name, address, phone number (NAP) consistency.

### Step 2 – Keyword Research

- Use Google’s Keyword Planner or free tools like Ubersuggest.  
- Target phrases such as “wedding photographer Flagler County,” “Palm Coast wedding photographer,” and “Flagler Beach wedding photography.”  
- Include long‑tail keywords like “affordable wedding photographer in Bunnell.”  

### Step 3 – Optimize On‑Page Elements

| Element | Action | Example |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Title tag | Include primary keyword and location. | “Wedding Photographer in Flagler County – Capture Your Big Day” |
| Meta description | Summarize services, add CTA. | “Looking for a wedding photographer in Flagler County? Get a free consultation today!” |
| H1/H2 tags | Use location‑specific headings. | “Why Choose a Flagler County Wedding Photographer?” |
| Image alt text | Describe photos with keywords. | “Bride and groom at Flagler Beach wedding” |
| URL structure | Keep URLs short and keyword‑rich. | /wedding-photographer-flagler-county |

### Step 4 – Build a Robust Google Business Profile

1. **Claim and verify** your profile.  
2. **Add high‑quality photos**: venue shots, candid moments, and behind‑the‑scenes images.  
3. **Encourage reviews**: after a wedding, send a short email asking clients to leave a review.  
4. **Post regularly**: share updates about upcoming shoots or wedding tips.  
5. **Use Q&A**: pre‑populate common questions like “Do you shoot at Flagler Beach?” and answer them.

For detailed guidance, see Google’s official help page: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177.

### Step 5 – Create Localized Content

- **Portfolio pages** for each venue (e.g., “Palm Coast Town Center Wedding Portfolio”).  
- **Blog posts** answering local questions: “Best Wedding Venues on US‑1” or “Why Flagler County is a Wedding Destination.”  
- **Client testimonials** with location tags.  

Each piece should include the target keyword naturally, local landmarks, and a strong call to action.

### Step 6 – Acquire High‑Quality Local Citations

- Submit your business to directories that rank in local search, such as Yelp, WeddingWire, and local chambers of commerce.  
- Ensure NAP consistency across all listings.  
- Use a tool like BrightLocal (https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/local-seo/) to monitor citation health.

### Step 7 – Monitor, Measure, and Iterate

- Track rankings for your target keywords using tools like Moz’s Keyword Explorer.  
- Monitor website traffic from local searches via Google Analytics.  
- Adjust strategy if a keyword’s performance dips or if new competitors emerge.

## Common Mistakes Wedding Photographers Make With Local SEO

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| **Ignoring Google Business Profile** | Without a verified profile, you miss map placements and review signals. | Claim, verify, and keep your profile updated. |
| **Inconsistent NAP** | Search engines see conflicting information and lower trust. | Use a spreadsheet to audit all listings; correct mismatches immediately. |
| **Over‑keyword stuffing** | Google penalizes sites that cram keywords into content. | Keep keyword density natural; focus on user intent. |
| **Neglecting mobile optimization** | Many couples browse on phones; slow sites lose traffic. | Use responsive design and compress images. |
| **Not asking for reviews** | Reviews drive trust and local rankings. | Send a polite review request after each wedding. |
| **Missing local citations** | Without citations, Google can’t confirm your local relevance. | Submit to at least 10 reputable local directories. |

Avoiding these pitfalls ensures your SEO efforts translate into real leads.
