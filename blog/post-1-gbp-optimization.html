How to Optimize Your Google Business Profile: Complete Checklist

Published: May 5, 2026 Reading time: 8 minutes

75% of local searches result in a visit or phone call, and 75% of those clicks go to the top 3 results on Google Maps. If your business isn't in that top 3, you're losing customers to competitors who are.

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the most powerful tool you have for local search visibility. Here's how to optimize it step-by-step.

1. Verify & Claim Your Business (Foundation)

First, make sure you actually own your listing:

  • Go to business.google.com
  • Search for your business name
  • Claim or manage your profile
  • Verify through postcard, phone, or email

Why it matters: An unclaimed profile means incomplete information = lost rankings.

2. Perfect Your NAP (Name, Address, Phone)

This is #1 priority. Google uses NAP consistency across the web as a trust signal.

✅ DO THIS:
  • Business name: Exactly as it appears on your legal documents
  • Address: Complete street address (not just city/zip)
  • Phone: A single, consistent number
❌ DON'T DO THIS:
  • "Joe's Plumbing" vs "Joes Plumbing" (inconsistent)
  • "123 Main St" vs "123 Main Street" (spelling variation)
  • Multiple phone numbers in the main line

Reality check: If your NAP doesn't match your website and online listings, Google thinks you're two different businesses. You lose ranking power.

3. Add High-Quality Photos (Critical)

Google prioritizes profiles with photos. Aim for 20-30 images:

  • 5-10: Your storefront/office (professional shots)
  • 5-10: Your team at work
  • 5-10: Your products/services in action
  • 5-10: Before/afters (if applicable)

Pro tip: Include photos monthly. Fresh photos signal an active business.

4. Set Up Services & Attributes

This is where Google understands what you actually do:

  • Go to Services → Add every service you offer
  • Add Service Areas (cities/neighborhoods you serve)
  • Fill out Attributes (wheelchair accessible, accepts online booking, etc.)
  • Keep Business hours current (including holidays)

5. Generate & Manage Reviews

Reviews are a direct Google Maps ranking factor:

  • Target: 20+ reviews in first 2 months
  • Ask customers: After purchase/service, text link: "Leave a review"
  • Respond to all reviews (within 48 hours ideally)
What to say in positive replies: "Thanks for choosing us! We hope to see you again."

What to say for negative reviews: "We appreciate your feedback. We'd love to make this right. Please contact us at [phone]."

6. Use Posts & Q&A

Google allows you to post updates and answer FAQs:

  • Posts: 2-4 per month (special offers, new services, events)
  • Q&A: Answer common customer questions (Google shows these in search results)

7. Add a Website Link (SEO Multiplier)

Link your GBP to your website. This:

  • Sends Google signals about what your site is about
  • Drives traffic to your actual site
  • Improves your site's local authority

Quick Wins Checklist

8 Items That Move The Needle

  • NAP consistency (website, GBP, other listings match exactly)
  • 10+ quality photos added
  • All services listed
  • Business hours set correctly
  • 5+ reviews generated
  • Website linked
  • Service areas defined
  • Q&A started

Complete these 8 items and you'll be in the top 50% of businesses in your area.

The Result

A fully optimized GBP = More phone calls + More inquiries + Higher visibility in Google Maps.
