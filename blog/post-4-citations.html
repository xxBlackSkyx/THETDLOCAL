Citation Building: The Most Underrated Local SEO Tactic

Published: May 8, 2026 Reading time: 9 minutes

Citations are mentions of your business on other websites (name, address, phone). Google uses citations as trust signals.

If you're only on your GBP, you're leaving 80% of your ranking potential on the table.

Why Citations Matter

Google asks: "Is this business real?" Citations are proof. The more places your business is listed, the more real Google thinks you are.

Citation count = Local Authority = Higher Rankings

Where to Build Citations

Top Tier

High Authority (Must Have)

  • Google My Business
  • Yelp
  • Apple Maps
  • Facebook
  • Better Business Bureau
  • Yellow Pages
Second Tier

Industry-Specific

  • Angie's List (home services)
  • Justia (legal)
  • Healthgrades (medical)
  • NAPFAM (financial)
  • [Your industry's main directory]
Third Tier

Local + General

  • Local chamber of commerce
  • City business directories
  • Neighborhood directories
  • Industry associations

Plus: Industry-specific directories for your niche

CRITICAL: NAP Consistency in Citations

Every citation must have the SAME:

  • Business name
  • Address (exact spelling)
  • Phone number

If you list "123 Main St" in one place and "123 Main Street" in another, that weakens your authority.

The Citation Building Process

  1. Audit current citations (search "[your business name]" across directories)
  2. List all missing platforms (compare to checklist above)
  3. Add NAP to missing directories (1 per day for consistency)
  4. Verify consistency (make sure every citation matches your GBP exactly)
  5. Maintain monthly (update hours, address changes, add new reviews)

Timeline

  • Week 1: Add to 5 top-tier directories
  • Week 2: Add to 5 second-tier directories
  • Week 3: Add to 10 third-tier directories
  • Result: 20+ citations in 3 weeks = immediate ranking boost

The Result

Clients typically see:

  • Ranking movement: Within 30-45 days
  • Click increase: 30-50% more clicks from maps
