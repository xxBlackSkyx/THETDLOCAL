# Local SEO Case Study: How We Ranked Pistol Pete's #3 on Google Maps in 60 Days

When Pistol Pete's contacted us, they had a problem every local business in Palm Coast understands: **they were invisible on Google Maps.**

Despite running a 5-star mobile detailing service covering Palm Coast, Ormond Beach, and the Hammock area, they ranked #12 for local searches. While competitors at #1-3 were flooding with leads, Pistol Pete's was getting lost in the noise.

**Here's exactly what we did—and the results.**

## The Problem: Invisible Online

Pistol Pete's had:
- ✅ A solid reputation (5-star reviews)
- ✅ Good word-of-mouth business
- ✅ A basic website
- ❌ **But ranked #12 on Google Maps**
- ❌ No Google Business Profile optimization
- ❌ No local citations
- ❌ No review generation system

**The reality?** 75% of local search traffic goes to the top 3 results on Google Maps. Ranking #12 meant they were losing leads to competitors every single day.

## Our Strategy: The 3-Step Local SEO Framework

### Phase 1: Google Business Profile Optimization (Weeks 1-2)

We completely rebuilt their GBP from the ground up:

- Rewrote business description with local keywords like "mobile detailing Palm Coast" and "car detailing Flagler County"
- Added 12 high-quality photos (team, before/after shots, service in action)
- Added detailed service descriptions for each offering
- Enabled messaging and appointment booking
- Created 4 initial posts showcasing services
- Added 15 FAQs addressing common customer questions

**Impact:** Visibility increased 30% within 2 weeks.

### Phase 2: Citation Building (Weeks 1-4)

Citations are like "votes" for your business. We built presence across 25 local directories including Yelp, Apple Maps, Facebook, Waze, YellowPages, BBB, and the Palm Coast Chamber of Commerce.

**Key principle:** Every single entry had identical NAP (Name, Address, Phone). Consistency is what Google rewards.

**Impact:** Local authority increased significantly. Google saw consistent signals about Pistol Pete's legitimacy across the web.

### Phase 3: Review Generation + Website Optimization (Weeks 2-4)

**Review strategy:**
- Created automated email template for past customers
- Set up SMS reminders 24 hours after service
- Responded to every single review (positive and negative)

**Website optimization:**
- Added LocalBusiness schema markup
- Optimized title tags: "Mobile Car Detailing Palm Coast, Ormond Beach & Hamcock"
- Rewrote meta descriptions with local keywords
- Added location pages for each service area
- Improved page load speed

**Result:** Generated 8 new 5-star reviews in 4 weeks.
